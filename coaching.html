Coaching & Connecting

We invest in leaders who are
investing in themselves.

Whether you are ready to move up, getting ready to move through something hard, or just ready to move on, I want to help you get there thoughtfully and strategically.

Moving up

You know what’s next. You want to compete seriously, present yourself well, and get there without making unnecessary mistakes. Getting ready, positioning yourself for what’s next.

Moving Through It

You are in the thick of things as an executive leader. You want to show up well in a complex organization and make meaningful impact in your current role. An executive coach is a great investment in yourself.

Moving on

Whatever the reason, you are in-between great opportunities right now. You are looking for help navigating the process of finding your next big thing and determined to do it right.

How we work

Getting Started

3 sessions minimum. Session minimum to begin. We assess and get deep and meaningful work done together—getting you ready, telling your story, targeting your search—mining for insights about where you are and how to get where you want to go.

Full Engagement

6–12 sessions for a more complete engagement arc, typically over 3–6 months. We do all of the above in great detail, iterating on your presentation and targeting while also connecting you to the right people and standing by you in a supporting role throughout your experiences as a candidate.

Custom and Long-Term

Lots of clients have their own way of making us a confidential sounding board, thought partner and coach on an ongoing basis. Getting that line into an annual budget on a recurring basis is the right investment in yourself. Weekly, monthly, just-in-time sessions when the unexpected happens or you have something big to prep for...whatever works for you is what you need.

What makes this different

Candor. Counsel. Contacts. Confidence.

I coach smart, accomplished leaders on their way to more. National nonprofit executives, senior insiders in government, COOs ready to be CEO and chief executives ready for next level opportunity. In every session, I will give you my candid perspective, tested counsel, and real contacts when they matter. In between sessions you will be working on exercises designed to get to the core of whatever needs to be done. You will leave each session and the process as a whole more confident about who you are and what comes next. Remember: We are seeking impact and advantage.
