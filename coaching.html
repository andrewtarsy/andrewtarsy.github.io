Coaching & Connecting

I invest in leaders who are
investing in themselves.

Whether you are ready to move up, move through something or move on, I want to help you get there—thoughtfully and strategically.

Moving up

You know what’s next. You want to compete seriously, present yourself well, and get there without making unnecessary mistakes. Getting ready, positioning yourself for what’s next.

Moving through

You are in it. You want to show up well in a complex situation and make meaningful impact in your current role.

Moving on

Whatever the reason, you are in-between great opportunities right now and looking for help navigating the process of finding your next big thing and doing it right.

How we work

3

Session minimum to begin. We assess and get deep and meaningful work done together—getting you ready, telling your story, targeting your search—mining for insights about where you are and how to get where you want to go.

6–12

Sessions for a more complete engagement arc, typically over 3–6 months. We do all of the above in great detail, iterating on your presentation and targeting while also connecting you to the right people and standing by you in a supporting role throughout your experiences as a candidate.

What makes this different

Candor. Counsel. Contacts. Confidence.

I coach smart, accomplished leaders on their way to more. In every session, I will give you my candid perspective, tested counsel, and real contacts when they matter. You will leave more confident about who you are and what comes next.

About Andy

This work comes from experience in every seat at the table. Running organizations. Sitting on search committees and boards. Raising capital. Advising founders and CEOs. And being an executive searching for a next role.

Andrew Tarsy has spent the last 15 years advising, investing in, and operating with growth-stage organizations. He’s been a CEO, COO, CFO, and Board member. Today, he spends his time making introductions and coaching leaders through transitions.

Many of the leaders we work with start with Emblem coaching and connecting while they are in transition, and bring us along once they’ve landed, to keep the momentum going and navigate what comes next.
